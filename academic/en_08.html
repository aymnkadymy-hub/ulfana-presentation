Comparison Methodology

How the comparison was conducted

  • Scope: Ulfana vs 17 platforms operating in or associated with Iraq, judged on 16 dimensions (category, AI, social, offline, dialect, transparency, privacy, scale, cost, …).
  • Confidence levels assigned per platform (high / medium / low); two “name traps” flagged (EduLink.Me is Estonian; Wajby is Saudi).
  • Honesty note: Ulfana’s facts are first-party (its own code/files); competitors’ facts are web-researched and labelled where vendor-reported.
  • X-algorithm facts: two independent deep web-research passes + direct repo fetches, with 3-vote adversarial verification per claim.
